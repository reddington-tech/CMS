# Modern Enterprise CMS Suite Walkthrough

I have successfully transformed your Cyber Management System into a premium business platform with a completely modernized UI, advanced security, and enterprise-grade operational tools.

## Key Overhauls

### 1. Modern Business Interface
- **Premium Dashboard**: A new executive landing page showing "Today at a Glance" stats with high-contrast Material 3 cards.
- **Fluid Animations**:
    - **Navigation**: Smooth slide and fade transitions between all screens.
    - **Entrance**: Daily records now slide into view one-by-one with a professional staggered animation.
    - **Charts**: Profit bars now animate their growth when you open the reports.
- **Shimmer Loading**: Replaced the basic loading spinner with modern skeleton shimmers for a high-end feel.

### 2. Enterprise Staff Management (Admin Only)
- **Centralized Control**: The public registration form has been removed to secure your business data.
- **Manage Staff Console**: A professional list where the Admin can:
    - **View Attendance**: Real-time status badges showing who is currently **Clocked In** (Green) or **Off Duty** (Gray).
    - **Update Credentials**: Edit staff **Name, ID Number, Phone, and Email** through a professional dialog.
    - **Create Accounts**: Add new employees directly from the management console.

### 3. Smart Operations & Inventory
- **Automated Supplies**: Track stock levels for Paper Reams and Ink. Dashboard alerts warn you in red when stock is low.
- **Service Breakdown**: Record and analyze revenue by specific categories (**Printing, Gaming, Typing, etc.**).
- **Professional Reporting**: Generate **PDF Business Reports** and **CSV/Excel Exports** with one tap.

### 4. Advanced Security
- **Locked Dashboard**: The app is completely secured behind a modern login screen.
- **Admin-Only Visibility**: Sensitive data (Profit trends, Staff management, Investments) is automatically hidden from regular staff members.

## How to Test

### Executive Dashboard
1.  **Login**: Use your Admin credentials.
2.  **Dashboard**: Verify the smooth entrance animation and real-time insights.
3.  **Clock-In**: Test the attendance system by clicking the shift status button.

### Staff Management (Professional Test)
1.  **Manage Staff**: Navigate to the side menu -> **Manage Staff**.
2.  **Verify List**: See all employees and their live duty status.
3.  **Edit Details**: Click the pencil icon to update a phone number or ID number.

### Reporting & Inventory
1.  **Supplies**: Check the **Supplies & Stock** screen to see your current inventory levels.
2.  **PDF Export**: Go to **Reports** and tap the PDF icon to generate your business summary.
