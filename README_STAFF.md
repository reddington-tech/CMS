# CMS Executive - Staff Guide

Welcome to the **CMS Executive** app. This guide will help you navigate the application and perform your daily tasks efficiently.

## 1. Getting Started

### Login & Attendance
- **Login:** Enter your registered email and password to access the app.
- **Clock In:** Upon arrival, tap the **"Clock In"** button on the Dashboard. This records your attendance for the day.
- **Clock Out:** Before leaving, ensure you tap **"Clock Out"**.

---

## 2. Shift Management

Your daily workflow revolves around your shift.

### Starting Your Shift
1. Tap **"Clock In"** on the Dashboard.
2. You will be prompted to enter your **Opening Balances**:
   - **Opening Cash:** The physical cash in the drawer.
   - **Opening M-Pesa:** The balance in the business M-Pesa account.
   - **Opening Till:** The balance in the business Till.
3. The system will notify you if there is a discrepancy compared to the previous day's closing balance.

### During Your Shift
- You can view your **Active Shift Status** on the Dashboard, including:
  - Time on duty.
  - Number of customers served.
  - Current personal collections (Cash vs. M-Pesa).

### Closing Your Shift
At the end of your shift:
1. Tap **"Clock Out"**.
2. Enter your **Closing Balances** (Cash, M-Pesa, Till).
3. Record any **Meals** or **Additional Expenses** incurred during the shift.
4. Tap **"Save Executive Summary"** to submit your records.

---

## 3. Daily Operations

### Recording Sales
- Use the **"New Customer Sale"** quick action on the Dashboard.
- You can toggle between **Services** and **Products** using the tabs.
- Add items to your **Current Basket**:
  - Adjust quantity and price if necessary.
  - View/remove items in the basket summary.
- Select the **Payment Method** (Cash or M-Pesa).
- Tap **"Complete Sale"** to finalize the transaction.
- **Note:** You must have an active shift to record sales.

### Recording Expenses
- Use the **"Record Expense"** button.
- Provide a description and the amount spent.
- Expenses are logged and associated with your active shift.

### Managing Supplies
- Access the **"Supplies"** menu to view inventory levels.
- You can manually adjust stock levels (+ or -) as items are used or restocked.
- Low stock items will be highlighted in red.

## 4. Performance & Records

### Weekly Performance
- The Dashboard displays your **Weekly Performance** stats, including Revenue, Net Profit, and Expenses.

### Reviewing Records
- Navigate to **"Business Records"** to see your shift summaries and individual transactions from the last 7 days.

---

## 5. Edits and Approvals

### Requesting Edits
If you make a mistake in a transaction or expense entry:
1. Open the transaction/expense details from your records.
2. Tap **"Request Edit"**.
3. Enter the proposed changes and a **mandatory reason** for the edit.
4. Wait for the Admin to approve the changes.

---

## 6. Security & Support
- **Logout:** Use the sidebar menu to logout when you are done.
- **Inactive Status:** If your account is deactivated by an Admin, you will not be able to perform business actions.
